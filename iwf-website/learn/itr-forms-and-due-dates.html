Income Tax guide

Which ITR form should you file, and by when?

ITR-1 to ITR-7 in plain language, with the due dates that apply after Budget 2026.

Last reviewed: November 2026 · Written by the IndiaWiseFilings compliance team

The form you file depends on the type of income you have and the kind of taxpayer you are. Filing on the wrong form can lead to a defective-return notice, so it is worth getting right.

FormWho uses it
ITR-1 (Sahaj)Resident individuals with salary or pension, one house property, other sources such as interest, and limited long-term capital gains, with total income up to ₹50 lakh
ITR-2Individuals and HUFs without business income, including those with capital gains, more than one house property, foreign assets or income above ₹50 lakh
ITR-3Individuals and HUFs with business or professional income, including freelancers and partners in firms
ITR-4 (Sugam)Residents who opt for presumptive taxation on business or professional income, within the prescribed limits
ITR-5Firms, LLPs, associations of persons and bodies of individuals
ITR-6Companies
ITR-7Trusts, political parties, research institutions and similar entities

Due dates

Budget 2026 separated the due dates for salaried and non-audit business taxpayers.

WhoDue date
ITR-1 and ITR-2 filers31 July
ITR-3 and ITR-4 filers who do not need a tax audit31 August
Taxpayers whose accounts must be audited, including all companies31 October (tax audit report by 30 September)
Transfer pricing cases30 November
Belated return31 December
Revised return31 March

Before you file

  • Match your income and TDS with Form 26AS and the Annual Information Statement (AIS).
  • Decide between the new and old tax regimes. Business taxpayers who want the old regime must opt for it on time.
  • Keep proofs of deductions, rent receipts and capital gains statements ready.
  • Verify the return within 30 days of filing, usually through Aadhaar OTP, or it is treated as not filed.
The Income-tax Act, 2025 has replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026, and form names and section numbers are being updated. Confirm the form and date that apply to you on the income tax portal or with a CA.
