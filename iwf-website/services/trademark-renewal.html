— About Trademark Renewal —

Keep your trademark in force for another 10 years.

A registered trademark in India is valid for 10 years and can be renewed indefinitely for further periods of 10 years. Renewal is filed in Form TM-R, ideally within the six months before expiry.

If you miss the expiry date, the mark can still be renewed within six months with a surcharge. After that, it may be removed from the register, and restoration is possible only within a limited time. We track your dates so this never happens.

  • No lapseRenewed before the expiry date.
  • Grace period handledLate renewal with surcharge if needed.
  • Restoration supportHelp if the mark has already been removed.
  • Portfolio trackingReminders for every mark you own.

— FAQ —

Questions people ask us.

When can I renew my trademark?

Up to six months before the expiry date.

What if I miss the deadline?

You can renew within six months after expiry with a surcharge. After that, the mark may be removed, and restoration is possible only within a limited time.

Do I need to show use to renew?

No. But a mark not used for five years can be challenged for non-use.

Can I change the mark at renewal?

No. Renewal keeps the same mark. A changed mark needs a new application.
